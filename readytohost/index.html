Questions

Good to know

Is booking direct secure?

Yes. Payments are processed through Guesty's secure booking engine, the same system that manages our Airbnb and Booking.com reservations.

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in from 3pm, check-out by 11am. Early and late options are available on request, with priority for direct guests.

Do you offer longer stays?

Yes. Stays of 28 nights or more receive a reduced rate. Get in touch for corporate and relocation stays.

I own a property. What does management cost?

We work on a transparent commission of booking revenue, with no set-up fees. Request an estimate and we'll share full terms.
